# Design Brief

The audit substitutes finite score32 throughput, aggregate embodied area, and
recomputed vectorless logic-plus-HBM energy into the existing quality-aware
frontier. The exact-FP16 row remains the measured arithmetic energy and
precision reference. Invalid mixed-int8 and abstract planning rows remain
visible only as excluded history.

No scalar weighting is imposed. Engineering Pareto points remain visible, but
promotion additionally requires structural quality. The current GQA8 hardware
cannot inherit that status from a Mistral-7B GQA4 generation test or from a
same-sharing synthetic proxy.
